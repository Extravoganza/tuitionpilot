    Questions students ask

    Is this real money?

    No. This is a competition prototype. The bank is a sandbox ledger and exchange rates are simulated, so you can explore safely. The calculators use transparent example fee schedules you can change.

    Can the AI send my money somewhere I did not approve?

    New payees always need your approval, and first payments to them wait 48 hours unless you verify the details by phone. Payments above your limit need a one time code that works only for that exact payment.

    Does it predict exchange rates?

    No, and nobody can. It splits the purchase into parts with hard dates, which cuts the risk of a bad day. Most of the saving comes from cheaper routes and lower fees.

    What if I want to stop everything?

    Press Stop. All actions halt at once and any pending approval codes stop working. Reading your balances still works.

    Which currencies are supported?

    The engine works with any pair. The tools include 15 currencies common among international students, from KZT and INR to CNY, EUR and GBP.

    Where is the code?

    Open source on GitHub with tests, logs from every scenario and a security self assessment. See the Team page.

    How it decides, in this order

    1. Which direction is the money going?
    2. Is there a checked profile for this university?
    3. Are any key rules out of date? Then nothing is allowed.
    4. For each method: does the source cover this university, direction and payer?
    5. Is it confirmed and still fresh? Unknown never counts as yes.
    6. Right currency, right account type, carries the reference?
    7. All documents in?
    8. Can it arrive before the safe date, counting weekends and holidays?

          Rule simulator

          Rules checked, in order

            Four permission levels

            You choose how much the agent may do. Click a level to load it into the simulator.

            Hard limits

            Per payment $15,000, per day $20,000, per 30 days $30,000, at most 60% of a need in one currency purchase. No approval unlocks them.

            One time approval codes

            Valid once, for 15 minutes, for one exact payment. Rules are checked again when you approve.

            New payees wait

            Adding one always needs you. First payments wait 48 hours unless you confirm the details by phone.

            Edits to history are detected

            Each entry carries the fingerprint of the one before it. Change one and verification points to it. Deleting the newest entries needs an exported checkpoint to catch.

            Where the saving comes from

            Routing and fees. Simulated rates are a random walk, so timing alone averages close to zero. Nobody can predict a random walk, and we do not claim to.

            What the plan really buys you

            Lower risk. Spreading the purchase over hard dates cut the spread of outcomes by 59% compared with one conversion at the deadline. Students who want certainty can lock everything on day one.
